# Home Additions: 12 frames

The sunroom gets built around the camera. Day one is the backyard where the
room will stand, with the house's back wall on the left. Then the slab, the
framing, the roof and the windows go up around you, until you're sitting in
the finished room. Your service photo is frame 12 (`keys/12.png`,
`frames/12.png`).

| Frame | Day | Crew action (for the crew prompt) |
|---|---|---|

| 02 | Slab poured | Add 2 workers smoothing the wet concrete with a long-handled bull float. |
| 03 | Walls framed | Add 2 workers raising a framed wall section into place. |
| 04 | Roof framed | Add 2 workers: one on a ladder setting a rafter, one on top of the wall nailing it. |
